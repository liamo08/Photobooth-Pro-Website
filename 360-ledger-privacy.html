360°Ledger — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026

360Ledger is an operator app for 360° spinning-arm video booths, provided by Photobooth Guys. This policy explains what data the app and its cloud service collect, how it is used, and the choices you have — including how to delete your account.

Who we are

360Ledger is operated by Photobooth Guys (Ireland). For any privacy question or request, contact us at info@photoboothguys.ie.

Information we collect

  • Account details — the email address and password you sign up with (passwords are stored only as a one-way hash, never in plain text) and an optional display name.
  • Event information — the events you create (name, schedule, branding and effect settings).
  • Captured video — the video clips and thumbnails recorded by the booth and uploaded to our cloud so they can be shared with the people filmed.
  • Billing details — if you pay for a plan, an event pass or SMS credit: your billing name, country, VAT number and address (they decide the VAT and are printed on your invoices), and the invoices themselves. Card payments are handled by Revolut; we never see or store your card details.
  • Guest contact details — a guest’s email address or mobile number, when they ask to receive their clip by email or text (see Guest contact details below).
  • Limited technical data — your IP address at sign-up and login, used only to secure the service and prevent abuse of the free plan.

How we use your information

  • To provide and operate the app and your account.
  • To process captured clips on the device, store them, and generate a share link so a guest can view and download their own clip.
  • To manage your plan, payments, SMS credit and invoices.
  • To send a guest their clip by email or text message when they ask for it.
  • To keep the service secure and prevent fraud or abuse.
  • To contact you about your account when necessary.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

Video clips and guests

Clips captured at an event are uploaded to our cloud storage and made available through a private share link (for example via a QR code shown after capture). The booth operator controls which clips are captured. Operators are responsible for obtaining any consent required from the people who are filmed.

Guest contact details

A guest can ask for their clip to be sent to their email address or mobile number from the booth. We use that address or number to send the link to their clip, and we keep a short log of each message sent (to prevent abuse). The destination in that log is removed after 90 days.

The event host (the booth operator or the person running the event) may also ask the guest whether they agree to the host keeping their contact details. The guest answers Yes or No — nothing is pre-selected — and gets their clip either way. The contact is kept only if the guest says Yes, and only if the host has this option turned on for the event.

  • Who is responsible — for contact details kept with consent, the event host is the data controller: they decide what the details are used for, and must use them only for the purpose explained to the guest. 360Ledger (Photobooth Guys) is the data processor, storing the details on the host’s behalf.
  • How long — kept contact details are deleted automatically 12 months after they were collected, or earlier if the host deletes them or the event or host account is deleted.
  • Removal — a guest can ask the event host to delete their details at any time, or write to info@photoboothguys.ie and we will arrange it.

Where your data is stored

Data is stored on servers located in the European Union (Hetzner, Finland and Germany). We use Cloudflare for DNS and security. These providers process data only to host and protect the service on our behalf.

We also use these sub-processors:

  • Twilio — delivers text messages to guests (the mobile number and the message).
  • Our email provider — delivers emails to guests (the email address and the message).
  • Revolut — processes card payments for plans, event passes and SMS credit (your email, the amount and the payment).

Some of these providers may process data outside the European Union; where they do, they rely on the safeguards the GDPR requires, such as the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses.

Data retention and deletion

We keep your account and its data while your account is active. You can permanently delete your account and everything in it at any time:

  • In the app — open the account menu (top right of your events list) and choose Delete account.
  • On the web — sign in at 360ledger.boothledger.com and use Delete account at the bottom of your events page.
  • By email — write to info@photoboothguys.ie and we will delete it for you.

Deleting your account permanently removes your account, your events, every captured clip and thumbnail, the guest contact details kept for your events, your message logs and your SMS credit history from our database and storage. This cannot be undone. Any monthly subscription is cancelled first.

Invoices are the exception: invoices and the payment records behind them are kept for the period required by Irish tax law (currently six years), even after your account is deleted. They are unlinked from the deleted account.

Free-credit abuse prevention: new accounts receive a one-off free SMS credit. To stop the same email address claiming it again by deleting and re-creating an account, we keep a one-way cryptographic hash (SHA-256) of the account's email address after deletion. The hash cannot be turned back into your email address, is not linked to any other data, and is used only to check whether that address has already received the free credit.

Your rights

Subject to applicable law (including the EU GDPR), you have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, and to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise any of these, contact info@photoboothguys.ie.

Children

360Ledger is a business tool for booth operators and is not directed to children. Where children are filmed at an event, the operator is responsible for obtaining consent from a parent or guardian.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS), passwords are stored as one-way hashes, and access to stored clips is controlled through expiring private links.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by a new "last updated" date at the top of this page.
