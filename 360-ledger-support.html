Help for the 360Ledger app on iPhone and iPad

Something not working, or a question about your account? Email us at info@photoboothguys.ie and we’ll get back to you, usually within one working day. Include the email address you sign in with and, if you can, a screenshot.

Getting started

  • Install 360Ledger, open it and create an account (or sign in).
  • Create an event, then open it and tap Start capturing. Mount the iPhone or iPad on your spinning arm, or use a Canon camera over USB.
  • Guests scan the QR code on the result screen to watch, download and share their video.

Plans and the TRIAL watermark

You can use 360Ledger free: clips then carry a small TRIAL watermark. To remove it, buy an Event Pass (one event, 72 hours from its first clip) or subscribe to 360Ledger Monthly (every event). In the app, open an event and tap Remove watermark.

Subscriptions bought in the app

  • Cancel or change: on your iPhone or iPad open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → 360Ledger, or in the app tap the account menu → Subscription → Manage subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge.
  • Restore purchases: account menu → Subscription → Restore purchases, signed in to the same 360Ledger account.
  • Refunds: App Store purchases are refunded by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

SMS credit

Texting guests a link to their video uses SMS credit (a few cents per text depending on the country). Your balance and Buy SMS credit are on the event screen when text sharing is on, and under account menu → Subscription.

Deleting your account

In the app: account menu → Delete account. This permanently removes your events, videos and settings. See the privacy policy for details.
