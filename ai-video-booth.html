The Short Answer

What Is an
AI Video Booth?

An AI video booth is a photo booth that turns each guest’s photo into a short AI-generated video. With BoothLedger it runs inside your normal booth: the guest takes their photo, picks a video style, and image-to-video AI animates it into a 5–10 second portrait clip that arrives on their QR gallery page. It works from the original photo or from an AI transformation, runs in the background so the booth never waits, and the print comes out as normal.

FAQ

AI Video Booth
Questions Answered

What is an AI video booth?

An AI video booth is a photo booth that turns a guest's photo into a short AI-generated video. With BoothLedger the guest takes their photo as normal, picks a video style on the touchscreen, and AI animates the photo into a 5–10 second clip — the group laughing, dancing, cheering under confetti, or morphing into a superhero. The finished video lands on the same QR gallery page as their photo.

Can I turn an AI photo transformation into a video?

Yes. When a guest starts an AI video they choose which picture to animate: the original booth photo or any of the AI transformations they made from it. So a guest can turn themselves into an anime character or a superhero with an AI photo effect and then bring that version to life as a video.

Do guests have to wait at the booth while the video is made?

No. The QR code appears straight away and the video is made in the background, usually in about one and a half to three minutes. The guest walks away, the booth is free for the next group, and the clip appears on their gallery page when it's ready. Printing is not affected — the photo prints as normal while the video renders.

How much does each AI video cost?

AI videos are paid from your BoothLedger AI credit balance, per clip. A 5-second clip costs $0.25 on the Value model, $0.39 on the Standard 1080p model and $0.99 for a 6-second Premium clip with generated sound. 10-second clips cost roughly double. Failed generations are refunded. AI video needs the AI Photobooth plan (€35/month).

Can I stop an event from spending too much on AI videos?

Yes. You can cap the number of AI videos per event, and set a credit budget per event in BoothLedger Cloud. When an event hits its budget, guests can't start new AI photos or videos, so a 300-guest wedding can't run away with your balance.

Can I write my own AI video effects?

Yes. Every event starts with 12 built-in video effects, and you can add your own from the event settings in BoothLedger Cloud: write a motion prompt, choose Animate, Style + Animate or Reveal, and save it to your video prompt library to reuse across events.

Will the people in the video still look like themselves?

That's the priority. Every effect runs with guardrails against face morphing, identity drift, extra limbs and flicker, and the Animate effects keep guests exactly where they stood. Motion is kept deliberately modest so friends still recognise each other.

Can I make AI videos after the event from photos already taken?

Yes. Open any session in the event gallery in the BoothLedger app and tap AI Video. The clip is attached to that guest's session, so it appears on the QR link they already have.

What format are the AI videos?

Videos are MP4 clips in the same shape as your photo, so a portrait booth photo becomes a portrait video ready for Instagram Reels, TikTok and Stories. Clips are 5 or 10 seconds (Premium is 6 seconds with sound) at 720p or 1080p depending on the model.

Which computer and camera do I need for an AI video booth?

BoothLedger runs on a Windows 10 or 11 PC or laptop with a Canon DSLR, mirrorless camera or webcam — the same setup as a normal photo booth. Videos are generated in the cloud, so you don't need a powerful graphics card, just an internet connection. See photo booth software for Windows.
