Before You Move

What to Check
When Switching

Your camera

Does the new software control your Canon over USB with live view, or do you need a webcam fallback? Test it on the camera body you actually take to events.

Your printer and cut

Check it prints to your dye-sub printer at the right size, including 2-inch strips if you sell them, without new drivers.

Your templates

Template files rarely move between apps. What matters is whether you can reuse your PNG artwork and rebuild a layout quickly.

Galleries and sharing

Is a gallery included, or is it another subscription? Can guests get photos by QR, email and SMS?

Patchy venue Wi-Fi

What happens when the connection drops? Shares and uploads should wait on the laptop and send when it's back.

The full yearly cost

Add booth software, gallery tier and AI together, for every booth you run, on the billing you'd actually use.

Migration Checklist

Moving from dslrBooth
to BoothLedger

  1. Install BoothLedger next to dslrBooth

    Download the free trial on the same Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) laptop. You don't need to uninstall dslrBooth, and no credit card is needed for the 30-day trial.

  2. Camera: close dslrBooth first

    Only one program can hold a Canon at a time. Quit dslrBooth (and Canon EOS Utility), plug in the camera by USB and open BoothLedger. If another photo booth app still has the camera, BoothLedger tells you which one. Check live view and a test capture, and set up a USB webcam as a backup if you carry one.

  3. Printer: pick your existing Windows printer

    BoothLedger prints through the Windows driver you already have installed, so your dye-sub printer (DNP and others with a Windows driver) carries over as it is. Choose the printer, print a 4×6 test, and if you sell strips, a 2-inch cut test.

  4. Templates: reuse your artwork

    dslrBooth template files don't open in BoothLedger, but your artwork does.

    • Find the PNG frames and backgrounds you made for dslrBooth.
    • Upload each one to the BoothLedger template editor as an overlay and place the photo slots.
    • Or start from the built-in catalogue and add your client's logo and wording.

    A simple frame with a logo is a quick rebuild; complex multi-layer designs take longer.

  5. Sharing and gallery

    Turn on QR, email or SMS sharing and check the event gallery on BoothLedger Cloud.

  6. Run one real booking

    Take BoothLedger to an event during the trial, with dslrBooth still installed as your fallback. Trial photos carry a small watermark, so a friends-and-family party or a booking you've told the client about is ideal.

  7. Move your bookings, then cancel

    Optional: put upcoming jobs into the CRM so each booking becomes a booth event with its template ready. When you're happy, subscribe and cancel your dslrBooth renewal.
