The Basics

What Is a Boomerang
or GIF Photo Booth?

A boomerang booth records a second or two of movement and plays it forward, then in reverse, on a loop. A confetti throw, a hair flick or a clinked glass becomes a clip that bounces back and forth. A GIF booth is the same idea saved as an animated GIF. In BoothLedger it is one mode on the booth, not a separate app or a separate machine.

Forward, Then Back

The booth records a short burst of frames, then joins them to a reversed copy so the movement bounces. The loop repeats for as long as the guest watches it.

MP4 or Animated GIF

Choose the output per event. MP4 is the default: smooth and small enough to send. GIF gives clients a true animated GIF that loops forever.

Digital, Not Printed

A boomerang is a moving clip, so it is shared, not printed. Guests get it by QR code, email or SMS, and it plays on loop in the event’s online gallery.

How It Works

What the Guest Does,
Step by Step

A boomerang from a photo booth: two guests with props, the movement playing forward and then back on a loop.

  1. Tap BoomerangThe Boomerang button sits on the start screen next to the photo button. Guests tap it once.
  2. CountdownA countdown runs (3 seconds by default, and you can change it) so the group has time to get ready.
  3. MoveThe booth records for the time you set, 1 to 4 seconds. The screen shows how far through the recording they are.
  4. Loop is builtBoothLedger joins the burst to its reverse and saves it as an MP4 or GIF. The guest sees it playing on screen.
  5. Send itEmail, SMS and QR code buttons sit under the loop. Each one sends a link to the clip. Tap Done and the booth is ready for the next group.
Settings

Boomerang Settings
You Control Per Event

All of these sit in the booth’s camera settings under Video and are saved with the event, so a wedding and a brand activation can each have their own.

Boomerang EffectOn or off. It is on for new events, so the button shows unless you turn it off.
CountdownSeconds before recording starts. 3 by default.
Record time1, 1.5, 2, 2.5, 3, 3.5 or 4 seconds. 2 by default.
Playback speed0.5x, 0.75x, 1x or 1.5x. Slower speeds give a longer, more dramatic loop.
Resolution720p or 1080p.
Output formatMP4 (default) or GIF. GIFs are capped at 15 frames per second to keep the file a sensible size, and loop forever.

If your camera is mounted in portrait, the boomerang follows the booth’s live view rotation, so the clip comes out the same way up as your photos.

Setup

Cameras and
What You Need

Boomerangs run in the same BoothLedger app as your photos, on a Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) PC. There is nothing extra to install.

Canon DSLR or Mirrorless

The booth records from the camera’s live view feed, which gives the smoothest loop. It is video quality rather than full still-photo resolution, which is normal for a boomerang. See DSLR photo booth software.

USB Webcam

Works too. The booth grabs frames as fast as the webcam allows within the record time, so expect a loop that is less smooth than on a Canon. Fine for casual and roaming setups.

Internet for Sharing

Each boomerang uploads to BoothLedger Cloud so guests get a link. If a guest types their email before the upload finishes, the email is queued and sent once the clip is up.

FAQ

GIF & Boomerang Booth
Questions Answered

Does BoothLedger make GIFs or MP4 boomerangs?

Both. Each event has a Boomerang Output Format setting: MP4 (the default) or GIF. MP4 is smoother and plays on any phone. GIF is a true animated GIF that loops forever, for clients who specifically want one.

How long is a boomerang?

You set the record time per event, from 1 to 4 seconds in half-second steps (2 seconds by default). The booth then plays that burst forward and backward as one loop, and you can slow it down or speed it up with four playback speeds.

Does the boomerang work with a Canon DSLR and with a webcam?

Yes. With a Canon DSLR or mirrorless camera the booth records from the camera’s live view, which gives the smoothest loop. With a USB webcam it grabs frames as fast as the webcam allows, so the loop works but is usually less smooth. See DSLR photo booth software.

Can I put my logo or an overlay on the boomerang?

Not on the clip itself today. Boomerangs are exported clean, without a frame or logo overlay. Your branding goes around the clip instead: the email subject and message, the SMS text, and the guest’s share page, which shows the event name in your event colour.

Can boomerangs be printed?

No. A boomerang is a moving clip, so it is a digital-only product. Guests get it by QR code, email or SMS. If you want a print as well, sell a photo session alongside it.

How do guests get their boomerang?

Straight after recording, the guest sees the loop on screen with Email, SMS and QR code buttons. Each one sends a link to the clip in the event’s online gallery. SMS can go by WhatsApp where that is set up. The booth needs internet to upload the clip; an email entered before the upload finishes is queued and sent once it lands.

Which BoothLedger plan includes boomerangs?

Boomerangs and GIFs are in the Photobooth plan at €12 a month (€120 a year); you do not need the Pro plan. Charging guests per session with pay to play, for example a Photo + Boomerang bundle, is part of the Pro plan at €35 a month. Both come with a free 30-day trial. See pricing.

Do boomerangs record sound?

No. Boomerangs are always silent, which suits a loop that plays forward and back. If you want sound, the separate Video mode records a forward clip and can record the microphone too.
