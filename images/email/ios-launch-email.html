What it does on an iPad

Live view with the iPad camera or a Canon DSLR

Print and share from one screen

Capture

✓Photos, boomerangs and short video clips from one capture screen.
✓Countdown and multi-shot sessions.
✓Photo filters, which guests can pick on the touchscreen.
✓Pinch to zoom on the live view.

Cameras

✓The iPad or iPhone’s own front and back cameras.
✓Canon EOS DSLRs and mirrorless cameras tethered over USB, with live view on the iPad.
✓DSLRs with a webcam mode also work as USB cameras on iPadOS 17 and later.

On Windows, BoothLedger also runs Canon and Sony cameras and webcams.

Printing, with no print server

✓Direct USB: cable a DNP (DS620, DS820, DS-RX1, QW410, DS40, DS80/DS80DX) or Citizen (CW-01, CW-02) dye-sub straight to the iPad. BoothLedger ships its own iPad printer driver, which you switch on once in Settings.
✓AirPrint: every other iPad, and iPhone, prints to any AirPrint photo printer.
✓Full-bleed 4×6 prints and 2×6 strips.

Direct USB printing needs an iPad with an M-series chip on iPadOS 16 or later (iPad Pro 2021 and iPad Air 2022 onward). That’s Apple’s requirement for printer drivers.

Templates

✓A ready-made template catalog to pick from.
✓A designer on the iPad: photo slots, text, logos, shapes and QR codes, dragged into place with your finger.
✓Templates sync from BoothLedger Cloud, so edits made on a computer show up on the booth.

Sharing

✓Guests send their photo by QR code, SMS or email, using large on-screen keypads.
✓Every photo lands in a hosted cloud gallery you can send to the host after the event.
✓On weak venue Wi-Fi the booth keeps shooting; uploads queue and send when the connection is back.

AI styles: a magazine cover from a booth photo

On This Date: an AI-written front page for a date the guest picks

AI (Pro plan)

✓AI styles and your own prompts: magazine covers, movie posters, art styles.
✓AI video: turns a guest’s photo into a short clip, delivered by QR.
✓On This Date newspaper: the guest types a date and AI writes a front page for it around their photo.

AI photos and videos use pay-as-you-go AI credits.

Operator tools

✓Set up events from anywhere in BoothLedger Cloud (or on the iPad), then sign in at the venue and tap the event.
✓Test mode keeps your setup shots out of the guest album until you go live.
✓A 4-digit operator PIN locks settings while you step away.

Cross-platform: one account on every device

One subscription and the same events, templates and galleries on Windows, iPad and iPhone. Every plan includes 2 devices.
