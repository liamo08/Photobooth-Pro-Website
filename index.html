Core Features

Everything You Need to Run a Professional Photo Booth

BoothLedger delivers everything professional event photographers need for unforgettable experiences.

Camera Support

Canon DSLR integration with live view preview, autofocus, and high-resolution capture. Built-in webcam support as a flexible alternative. More camera brands coming soon.

Beautiful Templates

Design branded multi-photo layouts with photo slots, overlays, text, shapes, and QR codes. Landscape or portrait orientation with cloud-synced template management.

Professional Printing

Direct printing to dye-sublimation printers like the DNP DS-RX1. Configurable paper sizes, auto-print mode, and multiple printer support for high-volume events.

Instant Sharing

Guests share photos instantly via SMS (Twilio), email, QR codes, or cloud gallery links. Customizable message templates with private share URLs for every photo.

Real-Time Filters

Eight stunning live filters — Black & White, Sepia, Warm, Cool, Vivid, Muted, and Vintage — applied in real-time to the camera preview so guests see the effect before capture.

Boomerang / GIF Mode

Capture Instagram-style boomerang animations your guests will love sharing. Configurable duration, playback speed, and loop count. Works with both DSLR and webcam.

Offline Resilience

Works seamlessly without internet. Photos store locally and sync automatically when connectivity returns. Upload queue with retry ensures nothing is ever lost.

Event Photo Library

Organize all photos by event with a browsable gallery. Portable folder structure makes backup simple. Track composites, raw captures, AI-generated images, and GIFs per event.

What is AI photo booth software?

AI photo booth software uses artificial intelligence to transform photos at events with effects like face swap, AI backgrounds, magazine covers, and artistic styles. BoothLedger includes two AI engines for creative transformations that wow your guests.

Is BoothLedger free to try?

Yes! BoothLedger offers a 30-day free trial with full access to all features including AI effects. No credit card required — just download and start using it.

What cameras does BoothLedger support?

BoothLedger currently supports Canon DSLR cameras with live view preview, autofocus, and high-resolution capture, as well as any USB webcam. Nikon and Sony support is coming soon.

Can I use BoothLedger for weddings and corporate events?

Absolutely. BoothLedger is designed for professional event photographers and photo booth operators. It includes custom branded templates, instant sharing via SMS, email, and QR codes, cloud galleries, and professional dye-sublimation printing support.

What AI effects does BoothLedger offer?

BoothLedger features AI face swap, AI background removal, AI art style transformations (magazine covers, pencil sketches, movie posters, social media styles), and more. Two AI engines: Core (best value) and Nano Banana Pro (premium quality).

Does BoothLedger work offline?

Yes, BoothLedger works seamlessly without internet. Photos store locally and sync automatically to BoothLedger Cloud when connectivity returns.

How much does BoothLedger cost?

BoothLedger starts at €12/month for the Basic plan (2 devices) and €35/month for the AI Photobooth plan with all AI features. Yearly billing = 2 months free. Start with a free 30-day trial.

What operating systems are supported?

Currently Windows 10 and Windows 11 (64-bit). macOS and Linux versions are coming soon.
