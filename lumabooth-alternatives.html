How Much Does
LumaBooth Cost?

LumaBooth comes as two main apps from the same company: LumaBooth for Windows (the product that used to be called dslrBooth) and LumaBooth for Apple for iPad and iPhone. Each is priced on its own, and each subscription covers two devices.

LumaBooth planBilled yearlyMonth-to-monthDevices
LumaBooth for Windows (dslrBooth)USD 17/mo (USD 204/yr)USD 49.99/mo2 included
LumaBooth for AppleUSD 18/mo (USD 216/yr)USD 19.99/mo2 included

From LumaBooth's public pricing page, checked 25 September 2026. fotoShare Cloud is included with a booth subscription. LumaShare is an extra USD 7.99/month and HashPrinter an extra USD 25/month. Check their site for current prices before deciding.

Is there a free LumaBooth plan? No. You can download it and test it for as long as you like, but every photo, GIF and video carries a watermark until you subscribe.

The catch on Windows is the monthly price. USD 17/month only applies if you pay a year up front. Month-to-month, LumaBooth for Windows is USD 49.99, nearly three times as much. That hurts seasonal operators who only want to pay during wedding season.

USD 49.99/month without an annual commitment is steep if you only work a few months a year.

LumaBooth's AI is a library of preset portrait styles. Operators who want face swap, custom prompts or AI video look elsewhere.

A Windows booth and an iPad booth are separate LumaBooth products, each with its own bill.

Sharing lives in another app

LumaBooth’s galleries and sharing run through fotoShare, a separate app and account, rather than inside the booth software.

LumaBooth is a mature product, and it's reasonably priced if you pay a year up front. But if you want more AI, simpler sharing or a lower monthly bill, that's exactly the gap BoothLedger was built to fill.
