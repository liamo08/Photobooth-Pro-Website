LumaBooth Pricing 2026

How Much Does
LumaBooth Cost?

LumaBooth is now three separate apps from the same company: LumaBooth for Windows (the product that used to be called dslrBooth), LumaBooth for Apple (iPad, iPhone and Mac) and LumaBooth for Android. Each is priced on its own, and every subscription covers two devices.

From LumaBooth's public pricing page, checked 25 September 2026. fotoShare Cloud is included with a booth subscription. LumaShare is an extra USD 7.99/month and HashPrinter an extra USD 25/month. Check their site for current prices before deciding.

Is there a free LumaBooth plan? No. You can download it and test it for as long as you like, but every photo, GIF and video carries a watermark until you subscribe.

The catch on Windows is the monthly price. USD 17/month only applies if you pay a year up front. Month-to-month, LumaBooth for Windows is USD 49.99, nearly three times as much. That hurts seasonal operators who only want to pay during wedding season.

Why Switch

Why Operators Look for a
LumaBooth Alternative

Monthly price on Windows

USD 49.99/month without an annual commitment is steep if you only work a few months a year.

AI is a fixed set of presets

LumaBooth's AI is a library of preset portrait styles. Operators who want face swap, custom prompts or AI video look elsewhere.

Three apps, three subscriptions

A Windows DSLR booth and an iPad booth are separate LumaBooth products, each with its own bill.

Different jobs, different tools

An iPad brand activation, a 360 spinner and a Windows DSLR wedding booth have different needs. One app rarely suits all three.

To be fair to LumaBooth: it's a mature product with the widest camera support of any booth app, and on yearly billing it's reasonably priced. If you're happy with it, there's no need to switch. The alternatives below make sense when one of the points above is costing you money or bookings.

The Alternatives

5 Best LumaBooth
Alternatives in 2026

1. BoothLedger

Best for Windows DSLR and webcam booths

BoothLedger is the closest like-for-like swap for LumaBooth for Windows. It runs on any 64-bit Windows 10 or 11 laptop, PC or Windows tablet with a Canon DSLR or any USB webcam, and prints to dye-sub printers. It was built by PhotoBooth Guys, an Irish booth hire company, so it's shaped by real event nights.

The difference is what's in the box. The AI Photobooth plan includes two AI engines (face swap, magazine covers, movie posters, sketch styles and your own prompts), plus AI video. AI generations use pay-as-you-go credits (from USD 0.06 per image), so you only pay for what guests create. BoothLedger Cloud galleries, SMS, email and QR sharing, and remote device management are included on every plan. It also has booth modes such as trading cards, newspaper covers, photo mosaics and data capture.

Price: €12/month or €120/year (Basic, 2 devices) · €35/month or €350/year (AI Photobooth) · full pricing

Pros

  • Cheaper than LumaBooth for Windows, monthly and yearly
  • Two devices included; month-to-month without a big premium
  • Face swap, custom prompts and AI video on Windows
  • 30-day free trial, no credit card

Cons

  • Windows only for now (macOS coming)
  • Canon is the only DSLR brand; Nikon and Sony users need a webcam or a Canon body
  • Newer, smaller user community than LumaBooth

2. Simple Booth

Best for easy iPad booths

Simple Booth is the best-known iPad photo booth app, and it also sells its own HALO booth hardware. It's easy to set up and has strong branding and marketing tools, but each plan covers one iPad, and extra iPads are paid add-on licences.

Price: Core USD 49/month or USD 490/year · Plus USD 99/month · Pro USD 149/month · week passes from USD 24 · AI at USD 0.10 per credit

Pros

  • Very easy for staff and guests
  • Week passes for one-off events
  • Optional all-in-one HALO hardware

Cons

  • iPad only; no Windows DSLR workflow
  • One iPad per plan, and extras cost more
  • Much pricier than LumaBooth per device

3. Snappic

Best for brand activations

Snappic is aimed at agencies and operators running branded activations on iPad: lead capture, surveys, analytics, microsites and white-labelling. Its premium AI effects and AI background removal sit on the Business plan. It supports DSLR and GoPro integration.

Price: Starter USD 69/month · Business USD 189/month · Premium USD 279/month · save 20% yearly

Pros

  • Best-in-class lead capture and analytics
  • White-label options for agencies
  • Polished iPad experience

Cons

  • Several times LumaBooth's price
  • The best AI is locked to the USD 189/month tier
  • iPad-first; overkill for weddings and parties

4. Touchpix

Best for 360 booths and mixed platforms

Touchpix runs on iOS, Android, macOS, Windows and Linux with one licence, and is popular with 360-spinner operators. Plans are priced by how many events you can have active at once rather than by device, and cloud sharing is included.

Price: PhotoPass €45.09/month billed yearly (€541/year) · MultiPass €90.20/month billed yearly · 14-day free trial

Pros

  • One licence across every platform
  • Strong 360 and slow-motion support
  • Unlimited devices per licence

Cons

  • Video features start on the higher MultiPass tier
  • Priced by active events, which adds up for busy operators
  • Most expensive yearly entry point here

5. Darkroom Booth

Best if you hate subscriptions

Darkroom Booth is a long-standing Windows booth app sold as a one-time licence. It suits operators who want to buy once and not think about it. There's no AI, and upgrades to new major versions are paid.

Price: USD 295 one-time · 10-day licence USD 45 · version upgrade USD 195

Pros

  • No monthly bill
  • Mature Windows workflow
  • 10-day licence for one-off jobs

Cons

  • No AI effects
  • No built-in cloud galleries
  • Paid upgrades between versions

FAQ

LumaBooth Alternatives
Common Questions

How much does LumaBooth cost?

As of September 2026, LumaBooth for Windows (formerly dslrBooth) is USD 17/month billed annually (USD 204 a year) or USD 49.99 month-to-month. LumaBooth for Apple and for Android are USD 18/month billed annually or USD 19.99 month-to-month. Each subscription includes two devices and fotoShare Cloud.

Is there a free version of LumaBooth?

No. LumaBooth no longer has a free plan. You can download it and test it for as long as you like, but every photo, GIF and video is watermarked until you subscribe.

What is the best LumaBooth alternative?

It depends on your hardware. For a Windows laptop with a Canon DSLR or webcam, BoothLedger is the closest like-for-like alternative, with AI effects and cloud galleries included from €12 a month. For iPad-only booths, Simple Booth or Snappic. For 360 and multi-platform setups, Touchpix. For a one-time licence, Darkroom Booth.

What is the cheapest alternative to LumaBooth?

BoothLedger Basic is €12 a month or €120 a year with two devices included, which is cheaper than LumaBooth for Windows on both monthly and yearly billing. Darkroom Booth is a USD 295 one-time licence, which only works out cheaper if you keep the same version for several years.

Is LumaBooth the same as dslrBooth?

Yes. LumaBooth for Windows is the new name for dslrBooth. LumaBooth for Apple (iPad, iPhone and Mac) and LumaBooth for Android are sister apps from the same company, each with its own subscription.

Does LumaBooth have AI effects?

LumaBooth includes a fixed library of preset AI portrait styles and AI background removal. BoothLedger's AI Photobooth plan adds two AI engines with face swap, magazine covers, movie posters, sketch styles and AI video, plus the option to write your own prompts.

LumaBooth vs Snappic: which is better?

LumaBooth is far cheaper and runs on Windows, iPad and Android, which suits owner-operators. Snappic starts at USD 69 a month and focuses on iPad booths for brand activations, with lead capture, analytics and microsites. If you run a Windows DSLR booth and want AI without Snappic's price, BoothLedger sits between the two.

Can I switch from LumaBooth to BoothLedger without losing my setup?

Yes. BoothLedger runs on the same Windows laptop with the same Canon DSLR or webcam and dye-sub printer. Rebuild your templates from your existing PNG overlays, then run the 30-day free trial alongside LumaBooth at one event before cancelling anything.
