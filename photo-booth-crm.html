FAQ

Photo Booth CRM
Questions Answered

What is a photo booth CRM?

A photo booth CRM is booking software built for photo booth businesses: it keeps your customers, quotes, bookings, payments, invoices and staff in one place. BoothLedger’s photo booth CRM goes one step further and links each booking to the booth itself, so the event, the client’s template and its settings are ready on the photo booth without re-typing anything.

Do I need separate booking software and photo booth software?

Not with BoothLedger. Most photo booth businesses run a booking system and a separate capture app, then copy each job across by hand. BoothLedger does both: the booking lives in the CRM and one click creates the BoothLedger event, with the client’s design applied, which syncs to the booth when it’s online.

Can customers choose their photo booth template when they book?

Yes. Every booking gets a private customer page where the client can browse your template designs, type their own wording (names, date, hashtag) and upload their logo or artwork. You approve it, and the design goes straight onto the booth event.

How do I take deposits for photo booth bookings?

Send a quote link: the customer picks their options and pays the deposit by card to book their date. Connect Stripe or your own Revolut Business account, or switch online payments off and record cash and bank transfers yourself. Balances can be paid from the invoice or the customer’s booking page.

Is there a Check Cherry or BoothBook alternative for UK and Ireland photobooth businesses?

BoothLedger is built by PhotoBooth Guys in Ireland, prices in EUR and GBP, supports Revolut as well as Stripe, and is the only one of these that also runs your photo booth: bookings become booth events with the client’s template and settings, so there’s no copying jobs between systems. See BoothLedger vs BoothBook →

Can I import customers and bookings from my current system?

Yes. Upload a CSV or Excel export and BoothLedger matches the columns for you (name, email, phone, date, package, price). Columns it doesn’t use are kept on the customer or booking, and an import can be undone.

Can I schedule photo booth staff and track their hours?

Yes. Assign staff and booths to each job, send a job request on WhatsApp that they answer with one tap, and see who’s working, who’s been asked and who’s off on the schedule. Staff log hours, mileage and receipts from their own private link, and BoothLedger works out what each person is owed.
