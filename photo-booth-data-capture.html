How It Works

Lead Capture Built Into
The Share Screen

No extra kiosk, no clipboard, no separate sign-up form. Data capture lives inside the moment guests already give you their details — when they text or email themselves their photo — and asks one honest question.

1. Guest Shares Their Photo

They tap Text or Email on the booth and type their own number or address on the touchscreen keypad — the same flow they’d use anyway to get their photo.

2. One Clear Yes/No Question

Before sending, the booth asks whether the event host may keep their details, in your wording. Two equal buttons, nothing pre-selected, and a fixed line confirming the photo is sent either way.

3. Only a Yes Is Saved

A Yes lands in the event’s Data Collection tab on BoothLedger Cloud with the contact, its type and the exact consent time. A No sends the photo identically and stores nothing at all.

GDPR Compliance

Built Around the Consent Rules,
Not Bolted On

Most photo booth lead capture quietly keeps every address a guest types in. That isn’t consent, and in the EU it isn’t a list you can lawfully email. BoothLedger was designed so each contact you collect is one you can actually use.

Art. 4(11) · Recital 32

Explicit opt-in, nothing pre-ticked

Consent is an active Yes. Neither option is pre-selected and no tick appears until the guest chooses — a pre-ticked box is not valid consent under GDPR, and the CJEU’s Planet49 ruling confirmed it.

Art. 7(4) · Freely given

Unbundled from the photo

The photo is sent whether the guest says Yes or No. The booth states that in a fixed line under the question that custom wording cannot remove, so consent is never a condition of getting what they came for.

EDPB 03/2022

Declining is as easy as accepting

Yes and No are the same size, the same text size and each a single tap. Yes gets the accent colour and the warmer label — the legitimate nudge — but No is never shrunk, greyed out or hidden.

Art. 7(1) · Accountability

Consent timestamped

Every contact carries the moment the guest tapped Yes, stamped on the booth itself, so you can demonstrate when and how consent was given if anyone ever asks.

Art. 5(1)(e) · Storage limitation

12-month automatic expiry

Contacts delete themselves 12 months after collection, with no cron job to remember. Deleting an event removes its contacts immediately.

Art. 17 · Erasure

One-click deletion

Remove a single contact or an entire event’s list from the Data Collection tab. Erasure requests take seconds, not a support ticket.

Art. 13 · Transparency

Your wording, per event

Edit the question and the explanation for each event so it says who keeps the details and what for. A wedding can promise photos from the day; a brand activation can mention offers.

Chapter V · Transfers

Hosted in the EU

Contacts and photos are stored on servers in Germany and Finland. No transfer outside the EU, and a published privacy policy you can hand straight to a client.

FAQ

Photo Booth Data Capture
Questions Answered

What is photo booth data capture?

Photo booth data capture (or lead capture) collects a guest’s email address or phone number when they share their photo from the booth, so the event host can follow up afterwards. In BoothLedger the guest types their own details to receive their photo, is then asked a separate Yes or No question about whether the host may keep those details, and only a Yes is saved. The contacts appear in a Data Collection tab on the event in BoothLedger Cloud.

Is BoothLedger’s photo booth data capture GDPR compliant?

It is built around the GDPR consent rules. Consent is an explicit Yes/No choice with nothing pre-selected (a pre-ticked box is not valid consent under GDPR Recital 32 and the Planet49 ruling), declining is exactly as easy as accepting, the photo is sent either way so consent is never a condition of the service, the consent moment is timestamped, contacts auto-delete after 12 months, and you can erase any entry or a whole event in one click. All data is hosted in the EU. See the full privacy policy →

Do guests have to opt in to get their photo?

No. The photo is sent whether the guest answers Yes or No. That separation is deliberate: GDPR requires consent to be freely given, so it must never be bundled with the thing the guest actually came for. The booth shows a fixed line under the question confirming the photo is on its way either way, and that line cannot be edited away.

Can I turn data capture off for a specific event?

Yes. Data capture has a per-event switch in the event’s Sharing Settings and on its Data Collection tab in BoothLedger Cloud, and it syncs to the booth within about a minute. Switched off, guests are never asked the question at all, sending is not held up, and nothing is stored. It is on for new events by default so you can offer it without extra setup, and off in one click for a client who doesn’t want attendee details kept.

Who is the data controller for the contacts collected?

The party the guest agreed to hear from is the controller of that contact list: you as the operator, or the event host if you hand the list over. BoothLedger stores and processes it on your behalf. That means the list should be used in line with what the guest agreed to, with an unsubscribe on anything sent, and deletion requests honoured. The tools for that (erasure, export, expiry) are built in.

How long are guest contact details kept?

Contacts are deleted automatically 12 months after they were collected. Before that you can delete any single contact or an entire event’s list at any time from the Data Collection tab, and contacts are removed when an event is deleted. A guest who wants their details removed can contact the event host or BoothLedger directly.

How do I get the collected contacts out of BoothLedger?

Every event has a Data Collection tab listing each contact, whether it is an email or phone number, and when consent was given, with an Export CSV button. There is also an account-wide Data Collection page that shows contacts across all your events with filters for event, contact type, date range and search, plus its own CSV export for importing into your CRM or mailing tool.

Can I change the consent wording guests see?

Yes. Both the question and the explanation underneath are editable per event in BoothLedger Cloud, so a wedding can promise photos from the day while a brand activation mentions offers. Leave them blank and the booth uses built-in wording translated into all 8 supported languages. Your wording should say who keeps the details and what for; the fixed line confirming the photo is sent either way always stays.

Does data capture still work if the venue wifi drops?

Yes. Consented contacts are queued on the booth and delivered to BoothLedger Cloud as soon as a connection is available, retrying automatically. The consent time recorded is the moment the guest tapped Yes, not the moment the upload succeeded.
