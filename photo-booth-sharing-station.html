The Basics

What Is a Photo Booth
Sharing Station?

A sharing station is a second screen, separate from the booth, where guests find their photos and send them to their own phone. The booth handles capture and printing. The station handles sharing. Two queues instead of one.

A shorter queue at the booth

Guests who stop to type a phone number at the booth hold up everyone behind them. Move that step to a tablet on a side table and the booth is free for the next group straight away.

More photos leave the room

Guests come back to the station later in the night, find shots from earlier, and send them on. A photo that reaches a phone gets posted. A photo left on the booth’s hard drive doesn’t.

A live gallery and slideshow

Between shares, the station can run a full-screen slideshow of the event with background music, so the screen draws people over instead of sitting idle.

How It Works

Cloud Mode or
Same Wi‑Fi Mode

BoothLedger Share connects to your photos in one of two ways. Cloud mode is the default. Same Wi‑Fi mode is for venues with no signal.

Cloud mode (recommended)

The booth uploads each photo to BoothLedger Cloud. On the tablet, open boothledgershare.photoboothguys.ie and sign in with the same BoothLedger account you use on the booth. Pick the event and its photos appear. No pairing and no shared network: the tablet only needs its own internet connection.

  • The gallery checks for new photos every 15 seconds.
  • QR codes link to the photo’s online page, so guests can scan on mobile data.
  • If the tablet loses signal, it can still show the photos it has already loaded.

Same Wi‑Fi mode (no-internet venues)

The Windows booth app runs a small local server and serves the Share app itself. Put the tablet on the same Wi‑Fi network as the booth, then scan the pairing QR on the booth or type its pairing code. The Share app opens in the tablet’s browser, straight from the booth. Nothing to install.

  • New photos appear on the tablet as soon as the booth takes them.
  • Only the booth’s current event is shown, so guests can’t browse an earlier client’s photos.
  • Viewing and downloading work fully offline: a guest on the same Wi‑Fi scans the QR and the photo downloads from the booth.
  • SMS and email still need an internet connection on the booth to send. If a photo hasn’t uploaded yet, the message is queued and goes out with the link once it lands.

Setup

Set Up a Sharing Station
in a Few Minutes

All you need is the BoothLedger booth app on a Windows PC and a second device with a web browser.

1. Open the Share Station tab

In the booth app’s settings, the Share Station tab shows the Share web address and a QR code for it. Point the tablet’s camera at the QR to open the site without typing.

2. Sign in and pick the event

Sign in with the same BoothLedger account as the booth and choose the event. One account covers the booth, BoothLedger Cloud and Share.

3. Choose the share buttons

QR code, SMS and email can each be switched on or off for the event. In BoothLedger Cloud’s Share app settings you set up how texts and emails are sent (see below).

No internet? Pair locally

On the Share Station tab, tap “Venue has no internet? Show local Wi‑Fi sharing”. Connect the tablet to the same Wi‑Fi network as the booth, then scan the pairing QR or type the 12-character pairing code. “Reset pairing” disconnects every paired tablet when you need to.

Guest View

What Guests See
on the Station

A photo grid, newest first. Guests tap their photo and choose how to take it with them.

QR code

A large QR appears on screen. The guest scans it with their phone camera and opens their photo to download or share. Nothing is typed and nothing is saved on the station.

SMS

An on-screen number pad with a country code picker. A leading 0 (as in Irish 087 or UK 07 numbers) is handled automatically. The guest gets a text with a link to their photo.

Email

The guest types an email address and receives a link to their photo. The address is checked before it sends.

Slideshow with music

Full-screen slideshow with fade, slide, scale or flip transitions, adjustable speed, rotation, fit or fill, and a filter to show only the finished templates or only individual shots. Pick an audio file on the device for background music.

Optional print button

Switch on printing in the Share app’s settings and a Print button sits beside the share buttons. In a laptop browser it opens the browser’s print dialog. Phone and tablet browsers can’t print from the web app.

Google review prompt

Optional. Turn it on in BoothLedger Cloud and add your Google review link. When a guest opens their photo link and taps Download, they see a one-time invitation to review your business, asked once per device.

SMS: pay per text, or bring your own Twilio

  • Built-in BoothLedger SMS: no setup. Each text costs a few cents, depending on the destination country, and comes off your BoothLedger credit balance (the same credit used for AI). With no credit left, texts don’t send until you top up. Failed sends are refunded.

Email has no per-message charge. Add your own SMTP server (Gmail, Outlook / Office 365, Amazon SES, SendGrid and others) in Share app settings so emails come from your own address.

FAQ

Sharing Station
Questions Answered

What is a photo booth sharing station?

A sharing station is a separate screen, usually a tablet on a stand near the booth, where guests find their photos and send them to their own phone. BoothLedger Share is the sharing station app: guests browse the event gallery and share by QR code, SMS or email while the booth keeps taking photos.

Do I need to install an app on the tablet?

No. BoothLedger Share runs in a web browser, so any tablet, phone or laptop with a modern browser works. In cloud mode you open boothledgershare.photoboothguys.ie. In same Wi‑Fi mode the booth serves the app itself, and the tablet opens it by scanning the booth’s pairing QR.

Does the sharing station work without internet?

Yes, in same Wi‑Fi mode. Put the tablet on the same Wi‑Fi network as the booth and pair it. Guests can view photos and, on the same Wi‑Fi, scan a QR code to download their photo, all without internet. SMS and email need the booth to have internet to send; if a photo hasn’t uploaded yet, the message is queued and sent once it has.

Is BoothLedger Share included in my subscription?

Yes. Share uses the same BoothLedger account as your booth and has no separate subscription. Every plan includes sharing by QR, email and SMS: Basic is €12/month and AI Photobooth is €35/month, both with a free 30-day trial and no credit card. Texts themselves are charged per message unless you connect your own Twilio account.

How much does it cost to send photos by SMS?

With built-in BoothLedger SMS, each text costs a few cents depending on the destination country and is deducted from your BoothLedger credit balance, the same credit used for AI. Failed sends are refunded. If you connect your own Twilio account in Share app settings instead, BoothLedger charges nothing and you pay Twilio directly.

Can guests see photos from other events?

No. In cloud mode the station shows the event you choose after signing in. In same Wi‑Fi mode the booth only exposes its current event, and the tablet has to be paired with the booth’s QR or 12-character code first. Reset pairing disconnects every tablet and stops older photo QR codes working.

Are photos saved on the sharing station tablet?

No. There is no download button on the station itself. Guests take their photo away on their own phone by scanning the QR code, or by receiving a text or email with a link to it.

Which booth software does the sharing station work with?

BoothLedger Share works with the BoothLedger booth app, which runs on Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit) with Canon DSLR or mirrorless cameras or USB webcams. Photos sync to BoothLedger Cloud, and the same account signs in to all three.
