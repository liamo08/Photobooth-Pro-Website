The Short Answer

What Is a Photo Booth App
for Windows?

A photo booth app for Windows is a program that turns a Windows laptop, PC or tablet into a self-service photo booth. It shows guests a live camera preview, counts them down, takes the photo on a DSLR or webcam, drops it into a branded template, prints it and sends a digital copy to their phone. BoothLedger does all of that on Windows 10 and Windows 11 (64-bit) and adds cloud galleries, remote event setup and AI effects on top.
