How It Works

What Is an Event
Photo Mosaic?

A photo mosaic is one big picture rebuilt out of hundreds of smaller ones. Up close, guests spot themselves and their friends in the tiles; step back, and the tiles form the chosen picture. BoothLedger builds it automatically from the event's photo booth photos — straight from the same gallery link guests already have.

1. Open the Gallery

Guests already have the event gallery from the QR code or link they got at the booth. A “Create Mosaic” button sits right beside Share and Download All — no app, no sign-up.

2. Pick or Upload a Picture

Two ways to choose the big picture: pick any photo from the album, or upload your own — an engagement photo, a portrait of the couple, a company or sponsor logo.

3. Built & Downloaded In Seconds

BoothLedger colour-matches every booth photo into the grid and blends the picture through the tiles. The finished mosaic appears in under a minute, ready to download in high resolution.

FAQ

Photo Mosaic
Questions Answered

What is an event photo mosaic?

An event photo mosaic is one big picture rebuilt out of hundreds of smaller photos. With BoothLedger, every photo taken at the photo booth becomes a tile: up close you see the individual guest photos, and from a distance the tiles form the chosen picture — a favourite shot of the couple, a company logo, or any photo a guest uploads.

How do guests create a photo mosaic with BoothLedger?

Guests open the event gallery from the link or QR code they were given at the booth and tap Create Mosaic. They either pick a photo from the album or upload their own picture, and the mosaic is built from every photo in the album within a minute — ready to view and download in high resolution.

Can guests upload their own picture for the mosaic?

Yes. As well as picking a photo from the event album, guests can upload any picture — an engagement photo, a portrait of the couple, a company or sponsor logo — and BoothLedger rebuilds it as a mosaic made from the event’s photo booth photos.

Does the photo mosaic cost extra?

No. The photo mosaic is included with every BoothLedger online gallery at no extra cost — no per-mosaic fees, no AI credits, no add-on module. It is part of both the Photobooth plan (€20/month) and the AI Photobooth plan (€35/month), and both include a free 14-day trial — see the pricing page →

How many photos does a mosaic need?

The mosaic unlocks once the album has 8 photos and gets better as the event goes on — a typical wedding album of 200–400 booth photos produces a rich, detailed mosaic. New photos are picked up automatically, so a mosaic made at midnight uses everything shot since the first one.

Can I turn the mosaic feature off for an event?

Yes. The mosaic is on by default for every event, and vendors can switch it off per event with one toggle in the BoothLedger Cloud dashboard — useful for corporate clients who want a locked-down gallery.

Is this the same as a physical mosaic photo wall?

It delivers the same wow moment without the hardware. A physical mosaic wall needs sticker prints, a frame, and staff placing tiles all night. BoothLedger’s digital photo mosaic builds itself from the booth photos and every guest can make and keep their own — and it still works after the event, from anywhere.

Can the mosaic be printed?

Yes. The mosaic downloads as a high-resolution JPEG around 1,900 pixels wide, which prints beautifully as an A4 or A3 keepsake, a canvas, or a thank-you card image. Many operators offer a printed mosaic as a post-event upsell for couples and corporate clients.
