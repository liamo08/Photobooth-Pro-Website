How It Works

How Guests Print Photos
From Their Phone

A print station turns every guest’s phone into the camera. You bring a Windows PC and a photo printer; BoothLedger handles the QR code, the upload page, the print queue and the printing.

1. Guests Scan the QR

The station screen shows a QR code. Print it on table cards or signage too. Scanning opens your print page in the phone’s browser: no app, no sign-up, no Wi-Fi password.

2. Pick Photos & Copies

Guests choose up to 10 photos from their camera roll, set how many copies they want and tap send. Big photos shrink on the phone first, so uploads are quick on 4G or 5G.

3. The Station Prints

The station PC picks up each photo within seconds and prints it on your photo printer. The guest’s page shows it moving from In the queue to Printing to Printed.

Features

Print Station
Software Features

Everything an operator needs to run QR code photo printing at a live event: control over what prints, how much, and when.

No App, No Wi-Fi Needed

Uploads go through BoothLedger Cloud, so guests use their own mobile data. The phone never has to join the venue network or sit on the same Wi-Fi as the printer.

Approve Before Printing

Turn on Approve each photo first and nothing prints until you’ve seen it. Ideal for corporate events, schools and brand activations where every print represents you.

Copy & Guest Limits

Cap the copies per photo (1–10) and the photos per guest, or set no limit. Limits are counted per phone, so one guest can’t empty the roll.

Live Queue & Reprints

The operator screen shows the queue, the photo printing now and recent prints. Reprint a missing photo in one tap, or remove anything from the queue.

Pause Without Losing Uploads

Changing the paper roll? Pause printing and uploads keep queuing. Or switch off new uploads and guests see a friendly Printing is paused message.

iPhone & Android Photos

JPEG, PNG, WebP and iPhone HEIC photos all work. Photos are turned the right way up automatically, so portrait shots never print sideways.

Your Printer, Your Settings

Prints go to any Windows photo printer using your BoothLedger booth’s printer settings. With a DNP dye-sub printer, the operator screen also shows the prints left on the roll.

Private by Design

Location and other metadata are stripped on upload, and print files are deleted 24 hours after printing. If the QR ends up somewhere it shouldn’t, generate a new one and the old code stops working.

Into the Event Gallery

Optionally add guests’ photos to the event’s BoothLedger Cloud gallery next to your booth photos, giving the client every angle of the night in one place.

Branded Guest Page

The guest page uses your event’s brand colour and shows your own message, such as a hashtag, a thank-you from the couple or a sponsor line.

FAQ

Photo Print Station
Questions Answered

What is a photo print station?

A photo print station is a printer at an event that prints the photos guests took on their own phones. With BoothLedger, guests scan a QR code, pick photos from their camera roll and choose how many copies they want. The station PC prints them a few seconds later. No booth, no camera and no attendant taking photos: the guests are the photographers.

Do guests need to download an app or join the venue Wi-Fi?

No. The QR code opens a web page in the phone's browser, so there is nothing to install. Uploads go through BoothLedger Cloud, so guests can use their own mobile data and the phone never has to be on the same network as the printer.

Is this an Instagram or hashtag printer?

It does the same job without the hashtag. Guests don't need to post publicly, own an Instagram account or wait for a hashtag feed to pick up their photo. They upload straight from the camera roll, including photos they would never post, and the print comes out at the station.

Which printers work with the print station?

Any photo printer your Windows PC can print to, using the same printer settings as your BoothLedger booth. Dye-sublimation printers like DNP are the usual choice for events, and with a DNP the operator screen also shows how many prints are left on the roll.

Can I approve photos before they print?

Yes. Turn on Approve each photo first and every upload waits in the operator's queue until you approve or reject it. You can also pause printing while uploads keep queuing, reprint a failed or missing photo, and remove anything from the queue.

How many photos can each guest print?

You decide. Set the most copies per photo (1 to 10) and a limit of photos per guest (or no limit). Guests can send up to 10 photos in one go, and the guest page shows each photo's status live: in the queue, printing or printed.

What file types can guests upload?

JPEG, PNG, WebP and iPhone HEIC photos. The guest page resizes large photos on the phone before sending, so uploads stay quick even on mobile data. Photos are turned the right way up automatically, so portrait shots don't print sideways.

What happens to guests' photos after printing?

Location data and other metadata are stripped when a photo is uploaded. Print files are deleted from storage 24 hours after the job finishes. If you turn on Add photos to the event album, guests' photos also appear in the event's BoothLedger Cloud gallery next to your booth photos.

How much does the print station cost?

The print station is part of BoothLedger Pro at €35/month (€350/year), which also includes the AI photo booth, AI video and integrations. The free 30-day trial includes everything in Pro, with no credit card required.

Can I run a print station alongside a photo booth?

Yes. Print Station is its own event type in the BoothLedger app, so you can run it on a second PC next to your booth, or on its own at events where guests would rather print their phone photos than queue for the booth.
