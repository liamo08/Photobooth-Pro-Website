BoothLedger — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

BoothLedger is photo booth software for event operators, provided by PhotoBooth Guys. This policy covers the BoothLedger booth apps (Windows, iPad/iPhone and Mac, including the app published as BoothLedger Booth) and the BoothLedger Cloud service. It explains what data we collect, how it is used, and the choices you have — including how to delete your account.

Who we are

BoothLedger is operated by PhotoBooth Guys (Ireland). For any privacy question or request, contact us at info@photoboothguys.ie.

Information we collect

  • Account details — the email address and password the operator signs up with (passwords are stored only as a one-way hash, never in plain text) and an optional display name.
  • Event information — the events you create (name, schedule, print templates, branding and feature settings).
  • Captured media — the photos, GIFs, boomerangs and video clips captured by the booth. When cloud galleries or sharing are enabled, media is uploaded to our cloud so it can be shared with the people photographed.
  • Guest contact details — if a guest chooses to receive their photo by email or text message, the address or phone number they enter is used to deliver that media. We do not use it for our own marketing. Where an operator has contact collection enabled for an event, the guest is also asked a separate Yes or No question before their photo is sent: whether the event host may keep their details, and what the host will use them for (operators may word this question themselves for their event, and it must state who keeps the details and why). Neither answer is pre-selected and the guest chooses one. Only a guest who answers Yes has their contact detail saved to the operator's account for the event host to use; answering No sends the photo in exactly the same way and stores nothing. Contact collection is enabled by default on new events and can be turned off per event in BoothLedger Cloud.
  • Device and diagnostic data — a device identifier used for licence management, and technical error reports (app version, device model, and connected camera/printer details) used only to fix faults.
  • Help assistant questions — if an operator uses the in-app help assistant, the questions they type and the answers, together with the app version and camera and printer status, so the answer fits their booth.
  • Limited technical data — your IP address at sign-up and login, used only to secure the service and prevent abuse of the free trial.

How we use your information

  • To provide and operate the booth apps, cloud galleries and your account.
  • To deliver captured media to guests via share links, QR codes, email or SMS.
  • To manage your free trial, licence and subscription.
  • To keep the service secure and prevent fraud or abuse.
  • To diagnose and fix technical problems.
  • To contact you about your account when necessary.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

AI features and the AI services we use

Some optional features use third-party AI services. Nothing is sent to them unless the operator has switched the feature on for an event. In the iPad and iPhone app, the person is also asked in the app before anything is sent, with the AI service named, and can say no.

  • AI photo effects, AI video and colouring pages. What is sent: only the photo the guest picks for the effect, plus the instructions for the effect they chose. No name, email address, phone number or other details are sent. Who it is sent to: our AI processing partner fal.ai (Features & Labels, Inc., USA), which runs the AI model for the chosen effect. Depending on the effect, that model is provided by Google, ByteDance, Kuaishou (Kling) or PixVerse. The photo is also screened by an automated safety check. How it is collected: the guest takes their photo, taps the AI effect they want and, in the iPad and iPhone app, first agrees to a notice naming fal.ai. How it is used: only to create the effect the guest asked for. The result is saved to the event like any other photo.
  • AI background removal. Where the operator turns on cloud background removal, each photo taken in the session is sent to fal.ai to separate the people from the background, and for nothing else. In the iPad and iPhone app the guest is asked before the session starts. If they say no, their photos keep their original background and nothing is sent.
  • Help assistant. Questions the operator types into the in-app help assistant, together with the app version and camera and printer status, are sent through our servers to Anthropic (Claude), an AI service, to write the answer. No photos or guest details are sent. The operator is asked in the app before first use.

We share this data with these AI services only to provide the feature you or your guest asked for. We do not sell it, we do not use it to train AI models, and we use these services under their business terms and data processing agreements, which require them to protect personal data to at least the standard set out in this policy. fal.ai and Anthropic are based in the United States; transfers from the EU are covered by the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

Photos, videos and guests

Media captured at an event can be uploaded to our cloud storage and made available through a private gallery or share link (for example via a QR code shown after capture). The booth operator controls capture and sharing. Operators are responsible for obtaining any consent required from the people who are photographed or filmed.

Where your data is stored

Data is stored on servers located in the European Union (Hetzner, Finland and Germany). We use Cloudflare for DNS and security, and Revolut to process subscription payments (we never see your full card details). These providers process data only to host, protect and bill for the service on our behalf. The AI services described above (fal.ai and Anthropic) process data in the United States.

Data retention and deletion

We keep your account and its data while your account is active. Event media can be deleted per event from the cloud dashboard at any time. You can permanently delete your account and everything in it by contacting info@photoboothguys.ie or from your account settings at BoothLedger Cloud. Deletion removes your account, events, templates and uploaded media. Guest contact details collected through the opt-in described above are deleted automatically 12 months after they are collected, and can be deleted sooner at any time by the operator from the event's Data Collection tab. A guest who wants their details removed can contact us at info@photoboothguys.ie or ask the event host directly.

Children

BoothLedger is a professional tool for event operators and is not directed at children. Guests of any age may appear in photos taken at an event; the operator and event host are responsible for appropriate consent.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct, export or erase your personal data, and to object to or restrict certain processing. Contact info@photoboothguys.ie and we will respond within 30 days. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Irish Data Protection Commission.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above.
