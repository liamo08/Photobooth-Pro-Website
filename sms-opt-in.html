How guests opt in to BoothLedger texts

Last updated: 21 November 2026

BoothLedger is photo booth software by Photobooth Guys Limited (Ireland). This page shows exactly where and how an event guest asks for a text message. Texting is optional. A guest never has to give a phone number to use the booth, take photos, print them or get them another way.

Step 1: the guest chooses how to get their photo

After taking their photo, the guest sees the Share + Print screen. SMS is one of several choices, and none is required:

  • Scan QR: scan a code with the phone camera to open the photo. No phone number needed.
  • Email: type an email address instead.
  • Print: take a printed copy.
  • Done: leave without sharing.
  • SMS: only if the guest taps it themselves.

Step 2: the guest types their own number and taps Send

Tapping SMS opens the screen below. The guest types their own mobile number on the touchscreen and taps Send SMS. Directly under the button, the screen says:

You'll get one text with your photo link. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out.

Tapping Cancel closes the screen and nothing is sent.

What the guest receives

One text with a link to the photo they just took, for example:

Here are your photos: https://boothledgercloud.photoboothguys.ie/p/Xk3f9QmZ2a
  • One message per request. No follow-ups, reminders or marketing.
  • Numbers are never bought, imported, shared or sold. They are only used to deliver the photo link.
  • Each number is limited to a small number of texts per day.
  • Reply STOP to opt out, or HELP for help. Help: info@photoboothguys.ie.
