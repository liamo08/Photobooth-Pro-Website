BoothLedger — SMS Terms

Last updated: 16 November 2026

These terms cover the text messages BoothLedger sends to event guests. BoothLedger is photo booth software operated by Photobooth Guys Limited (Ireland, VAT IE4426171QH), 77 Camden Street Lower, Dublin D02 XE80, Ireland.

What the messages are

At an event using a BoothLedger photo booth, a guest can tap Send Photo via SMS on the booth screen and type in their own mobile number. We then send that number a text containing a link to the photo the guest just took, for example: "Here are your photos: https://boothledgercloud.photoboothguys.ie/p/…".

  • A text is only sent when a guest enters their number and asks for it.
  • Each request sends one message. Frequency depends on how many photos you ask to receive; numbers are limited to a small number of texts per day.
  • We never send marketing or promotional texts, and we never sell, rent or share phone numbers with third parties for marketing.

Cost

BoothLedger does not charge guests for these texts. Message and data rates may apply, depending on your mobile plan.

Opting out and help

  • Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving texts from us. You will get one confirmation and no further messages.
  • Reply HELP for help, or email info@photoboothguys.ie.

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Your data

The phone number you enter is used only to deliver your photo link. See our Privacy Policy for how we handle guest contact details and photos.
