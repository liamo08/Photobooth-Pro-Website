The Short Answer

Can You Use a Sony Camera
for a Photo Booth?

Yes. BoothLedger controls Sony cameras through Sony’s own Camera Remote SDK, over a USB cable. The camera streams live view to the booth screen, BoothLedger focuses and takes each photo, and the full-resolution JPEG reaches the PC in about a second — ready for your template, the printer and the guest’s phone.

Two things decide whether your Sony works: it has to be on Sony’s Camera Remote SDK list (below), and its USB Connection has to be set to PC Remote. No Imaging Edge, capture card or HDMI needed.

Setup

How to Tether a Sony Camera
to Your Photo Booth

What you need

  • A Windows 10 or 11 PC (64-bit) — laptop, mini PC or Windows tablet. See photo booth software for Windows.
  • A supported Sony camera on its latest firmware.
  • A good USB cable for the camera’s USB port, plus a spare.
  • Mains power for the camera — a Sony dummy battery (DC coupler) so it lasts the whole event.
  • A dye-sub printer such as a DNP, if guests get prints.
  • A touch screen and lighting — an LED panel or ring light keeps autofocus quick in dark venues.

How to set it up

  1. Install BoothLedger and start the free 30-day trial. The installer includes Sony’s USB driver.
  2. On the camera: set USB Connection (USB Connection Mode) to PC Remote, and Still Img. Save Dest. to PC+Camera or PC Only.
  3. Close Sony Imaging Edge (Desktop, Remote or Webcam) — only one program can control the camera.
  4. Plug in by USB. BoothLedger finds the Sony and live view appears within a few seconds.
  5. Add your template and printer, then run a test batch of shots and prints before the event.

Can I use a Sony camera for a photo booth?

Which Sony cameras work with BoothLedger?

Sony cameras supported by the Camera Remote SDK: the Alpha 1, Alpha 1 II, Alpha 9 II and 9 III, Alpha 7 IV, Alpha 7 V, Alpha 7R IV, 7R IVA and 7R V, Alpha 7S III, Alpha 7C, 7C II and 7CR, Alpha 6700, ZV-E1, ZV-E10 II, the FX3, FX3A, FX30 and FX2 Cinema Line cameras, and the RX0 II and RX1R III. Not in BoothLedger. How do I set up a Sony camera for tethering?

Update the camera's firmware, then in its menu set USB Connection (USB Connection Mode) to PC Remote and Still Img. Save Dest. to PC+Camera or PC Only. Close Sony Imaging Edge if it's running, plug the camera in by USB and select it in BoothLedger. The BoothLedger installer includes Sony's USB driver.

Do I need Sony Imaging Edge to use my Sony camera in a photo booth?

No. BoothLedger controls the camera itself, so Imaging Edge Desktop, Remote and Webcam aren't needed. Close them before you start: only one program can control the camera at a time.

Can I use a Sony and a Canon camera with the same licence?

Can I use a Sony mirrorless camera on a laptop photo booth?
