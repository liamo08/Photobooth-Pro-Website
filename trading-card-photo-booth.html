How It Works

What Is a Trading Card
Photo Booth?

A trading card photo booth is a live-event experience that turns every guest into their own collectible card. They step in, type their name, pose, and within seconds a printed, personalised trading card is in their hand. BoothLedger handles every step — capture, AI background removal, template, print and digital share — from a single Windows app.

1. Type the Name

Guests enter their name (and any extra fields you’ve set up — nickname, team, role, stats, table number) directly on the touchscreen. Text drops straight into the card template, so every card is unique.

2. Capture & Cut Out

A Canon DSLR (or webcam) takes the shot. BoothLedger’s AI background removal lifts the guest off whatever’s behind them — no green screen required — and drops them onto your branded card template.

3. Print & Share

The card prints on a dye-sub photo printer in 8–15 seconds and slides into a premium magnetic acrylic case. Guests also get a digital copy by SMS, email or QR — perfect for socials and your cloud gallery.

Questions Answered

What is a trading card photo booth?

A trading card photo booth is a live-event experience where guests step in front of a camera, type their name (and any optional stats or role), and walk away with a printed, personalised trading card of themselves — sealed in a premium acrylic case. BoothLedger captures the photo, removes the background with AI, drops the guest onto a branded template and prints the card on-site in seconds.

Can each guest add their own name to the trading card?

Yes. BoothLedger lets every guest type their own name and additional fields (nickname, team, role, position, stats, table number) directly on the touchscreen. The text renders straight into the template, so every card is unique even when hundreds of guests use the booth at the same event.

Do I need a green screen for the trading card photo booth?

No. BoothLedger’s AI background removal detects the guest automatically and lifts them off any backdrop — venue wall, branded backdrop, festival crowd, whatever’s behind them. You can still use a traditional green screen if you prefer; both methods drop the guest cleanly onto the card template.

Are the trading cards printed on-site?

Yes. Cards print on a dye-sublimation photo printer in 8–15 seconds and slide into a magnetic acrylic case for a take-home collectible. Guests also receive a digital copy by SMS, email or QR code for instant social sharing and the gallery is hosted online for post-event downloads.

What events are trading card photo booths good for?

Sports fan zones, brand activations, corporate launches, conferences, weddings, hen and stag parties, school formals, charity galas, comic-cons, gaming and esports. Anywhere a personalised, take-home keepsake adds more value than a paper strip print.

Can the trading card template be branded for our event or sponsor?

Yes. Every element is customisable — frame colour, front overlay, logo placement, the card back, the font, and the text fields guests fill in. We can match your brand kit or sponsor pack and load multiple templates so different sponsors get different cards at the same event.

What camera does the trading card photo booth use?

BoothLedger supports Canon DSLR and mirrorless cameras for the sharpest cards, plus webcams for simpler setups or roaming booths. See DSLR photo booth software → for the full camera list. Canon tethered capture gives the best image quality for clean background removal.

How big is the printed trading card?

Standard print size is 2.5″ × 3.5″ (63mm × 88mm) — the classic sports/TCG card size that fits inside the magnetic acrylic cases. BoothLedger can also be configured to print larger 4×6 or 5×7 card variants if you prefer.

Can guests get a digital copy as well as the printed card?

Yes. Every card is shared digitally as well as printed. Guests can grab it by SMS, email or a QR code at the booth, and the card lands in a hosted gallery on BoothLedger Cloud so they can re-download or share it later.
