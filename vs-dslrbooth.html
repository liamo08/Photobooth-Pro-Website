Magazine covers, vogue, sketch, movie poster looks, custom prompts and AI video — all on the Pro plan, using pay-as-you-go AI credits. dslrBooth sells AI portraits through separate AI credit plans starting at USD 14.99/month (about USD 0.10 per portrait).

Cloud-First Architecture

Cheaper Month-to-Month

BoothLedger includes 2 device licenses at EUR 12/month with no annual commitment. dslrBooth (LumaBooth for Windows) is USD 49.99/month unless you pay a full year up front.

One Vendor, One Bill

Software, cloud, AI, remote config, and printing are all in one subscription. dslrBooth splits galleries into fotoShare Cloud and AI into separate credit plans.

Common Questions

Is BoothLedger a good dslrBooth alternative?

Yes. Same Canon DSLR control, same instant printing, same custom templates — plus built-in AI effects, cloud galleries, and remote management at a lower starting price for multi-device operators.

What does BoothLedger include that dslrBooth does not?

BoothLedger Cloud galleries, a booking CRM and remote device management on every plan. The Pro plan adds every event type, AI background removal, magazine cover and art-style effects, custom prompts and AI video. dslrBooth sells galleries through fotoShare Cloud and AI portraits through separate AI credit plans from USD 14.99/month.

Is BoothLedger cheaper than dslrBooth?

For most operators running 2+ booths with AI and cloud, yes. BoothLedger bundles what dslrBooth charges for as separate add-ons.

Can I migrate from dslrBooth to BoothLedger?

Yes. BoothLedger supports the same Canon DSLRs and dye-sub printers (DNP, HiTi). Run a free 30-day trial alongside your current setup at one event before switching.

Does dslrBooth still exist?

Yes. dslrBooth is now sold as LumaBooth for Windows, and dslrbooth.com still runs. BoothLedger is a direct competitor on Windows, with cloud galleries on every plan and AI on the Pro plan.

How long does the migration from dslrBooth take?

Most operators are up and running on BoothLedger inside an hour. Plug in your Canon, point BoothLedger at your existing template assets, configure your printer, and you're ready to run a real event on the 30-day free trial.
