Why DSLR Operators Choose

True DSLR Image Quality

Direct USB tethering to Canon DSLRs means full-resolution RAW or JPEG straight off the sensor. Snappic relies on iPad camera or AirPlay-bridged DSLR, which compresses images and adds latency.

Offline-First Reliability

Venues with bad WiFi don't break BoothLedger. Photos save locally and sync to BoothLedger Cloud when connectivity returns. Snappic leans heavily on online services.

Professional Print Pipeline

Direct DNP and HiTi dye-sub printer support over USB, with full layout control. Snappic uses a separate print server bridge that adds setup complexity. The BoothLedger iPad app prints the same way, straight down the USB cable to the dye-sub.

Half the Price for 2 Booths

EUR 12/month covers two device licenses on BoothLedger. Snappic charges per device, so a 2-iPad operator typically pays double.

FAQ

BoothLedger vs Snappic
Common Questions

Is BoothLedger a good Snappic alternative?

Yes — especially if you want native Canon DSLR quality, offline reliability, and direct dye-sub printing. Snappic is iPad-first; BoothLedger is Windows + DSLR-first with cloud built in.

What is the main difference between BoothLedger and Snappic?

Platform and camera. Snappic runs on iPad with the iPad camera (or an AirPlay-bridged DSLR). BoothLedger runs on Windows with direct Canon DSLR tethering for full-resolution capture, which gives more control over focus, exposure and live view, plus native dye-sub printer support.

Is BoothLedger cheaper than Snappic?

For operators running 2+ booths with AI and printing, yes. BoothLedger bundles 2 device licenses from EUR 12/month. Snappic plans typically start higher per device and have separate AI add-on costs.

Why is BoothLedger Windows instead of iPad?

Windows is where BoothLedger started: direct Canon DSLR tethering and full offline reliability are strongest there, and a tethered DSLR still beats a tablet camera for printed event work. A BoothLedger app for iPad and iPhone is coming, and it keeps native printing — it drives DNP and Citizen dye-subs straight over USB rather than through AirPrint or a print-server bridge.

Does BoothLedger have AI photo effects like Snappic?

Yes — AI magazine covers, art styles, custom prompts, vogue, sketch, movie poster and background removal, with two engine tiers (value and premium).
